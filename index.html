About

I am a PhD economist whose work draws on behavioral economics, experimental economics, and neuroeconomics, along with psychology, philosophy, and religion, to build a multidisciplinary understanding of human choice behavior. I completed my doctoral studies at the University of Maryland and have since taught and researched at NYU, the University of Pennsylvania, and Rutgers University.
